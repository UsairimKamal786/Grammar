# Day 12 – Prepositions

Prepositions show **time, place, or direction**.  
Small words, big impact.

---

## 1. Common Prepositions

| Preposition | Use | Example |
|-------------|-----|---------|
| in | months, years, countries | I was born in 2010. |
| on | days, dates, surfaces | The book is on the table. |
| at | exact time, place | Meet me at 5 PM. |
| by | agent or deadline | The work was done by Ali. |

---
