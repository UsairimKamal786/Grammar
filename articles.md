# Day 04 – Articles (A, An, The)

## 1. What are Articles?
Articles are words used **before nouns** to show whether something is general or specific.

There are **three articles** in English:
- a
- an
- the

---

## 2. Indefinite Articles: A & An
Used when we talk about something **not specific** or **for the first time**.

### Use **A** before words that start with a **consonant sound**
- a book
- a car
- a student

**Example sentence:**
> I have a laptop.

---

### Use **An** before words that start with a **vowel sound**
- an apple
- an hour
- an umbrella

⚠️ Important: It’s about **sound**, not spelling.

**Example:**
> He waited for an hour.  
(`hour` starts with a vowel sound)

---

## 3. Definite Article: The
Used when we talk about something **specific** or **already known**.

**Examples:**
- the sun
- the teacher
- the computer on the desk

**Example sentence:**
> The laptop on the table is mine.

---

## 4. When NOT to Use Articles
We usually do NOT use articles with:
- plural nouns (general meaning)
- uncountable nouns

**Examples:**
- I like cats.
- She loves music.
- We drink water.

❌ I like the cats (general)  
✅ I like cats

---

## 5. Common Article Mistakes

❌ He is a honest boy  
✅ He is **an** honest boy

❌ She bought the book (first time)  
✅ She bought **a** book

❌ I like the music (general)  
✅ I like music
