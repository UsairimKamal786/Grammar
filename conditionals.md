# Day 13 – Advanced Conditionals

Conditionals express **possibilities, hypotheticals, and results**.

---

## 1. Review: Types of Conditionals
- Zero: general truths → If + present, present  
- First: real future → If + present, will + verb  
- Second: unreal present → If + past, would + verb  
- Third: unreal past → If + past perfect, would have + past participle  

---

## 2. Mixed Conditionals
- Combine different times in one sentence

**Examples:**  
- If I had studied harder (past), I would be successful now (present).  
- If she were richer (present), she would have bought that house (past).  

---

## 3. Inverted Conditionals (Formal)
- Drop “if” + invert auxiliary

**Example:**  
- Had I known, I would have helped.  
- Were I taller, I would play basketball.

---

## 4. Practice Section

1. Write a first conditional about studying.  
2. Write a second conditional about being rich.  
3. Write a mixed conditional about a past mistake.  
4. Convert “If I had seen her, I would have spoken” to inverted form.  

---
