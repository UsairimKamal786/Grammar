Day 18 Grammar: Past Modals (Regrets & Possibilities in the Past) Past modals are used to talk about things that could have, should have, or might have happened but didn’t, or to show past probability. 1. Common Past Modals Modal Use Example should have + past participle Regret / Advice in the past I should have studied harder. could have + past participle Past ability / Possibility She could have won the race if she tried. might have + past participle Past possibility He might have missed the bus. would have + past participle Hypothetical past I would have gone if I had time. 2. Quick Tips Always use past participle after the modal: ✅ He should have eaten breakfast. ❌ He should have eat breakfast. ❌ Negative form: modal + not + have + past participle: I should not have lied. She could not have finished so fast. Questions: modal + subject + have + past participle? Could you have done it faster? Should we have told them? 3. Mini Exercise Fill in the blanks with the correct past modal: I ___ (should / have / studied) for the exam, but I didn’t. She ___ (might / have / missed) the bus this morning. We ___ (could / have / solved) the problem if we worked together. He ___ (would / have / helped) you if you asked.
